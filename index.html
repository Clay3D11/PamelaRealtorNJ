        New Jersey rules to know

        3-day attorney review

        After both sides sign the contract, either side's attorney can cancel or change it within 3 business days. Most New Jersey buyers hire a real estate attorney.

        Mansion tax: now paid by the seller

        Since July 10, 2025, the Graduated Percent Fee on homes over $1M (1% to 3.5% of the price) is paid by the seller, not the buyer.

        Realty Transfer Fee: seller pays

        New Jersey's transfer fee is a seller cost, so it is not part of your cash to close as a buyer.

        2026 loan limits

        In Essex County a conforming loan can go up to $1,209,750 for a single-family home. Bigger loans need a jumbo mortgage.

        NJHMFA down payment help

        First-time buyers may get up to $15,000 (up to $22,000 for first-generation buyers) as a loan forgiven after 5 years, paired with an NJHMFA first mortgage. Amounts and income limits vary.

        Property taxes vary by town

        Town rates in this calculator are approximate 2025 effective rates. Always check the home's real tax bill. Eligible owners can apply for ANCHOR property tax relief.

        *Estimates for education only, not a loan offer. Rates are samples; your actual rate, PMI/MIP, fees, taxes, and NJHMFA eligibility depend on your credit, lender, and property. Cash to close uses typical New Jersey buyer closing costs (see the NJ Closing Costs tab).

        Good questions

        Answers before you even ask.

        How much do I need for a down payment in New Jersey?

        Less than most people think. FHA loans start at 3.5% down, some conventional loans at 3%, and VA loans can be 0% down. New Jersey also helps first-time buyers through NJHMFA (New Jersey Housing and Mortgage Finance Agency) down payment assistance programs. Pamela can tell you which ones you may qualify for.

        Does it cost anything to work with Pamela as a buyer?

        The first consultation is always free. Buyer agent pay is now agreed on up front in a written buyer agreement, and it's often paid by the seller as part of the deal. Pamela walks you through every option clearly, before you sign anything.

        How long does it take to buy a home?

        In New Jersey, once both sides sign the contract there's a 3-business-day attorney review. After that, closing usually takes 30 to 60 days. Getting pre-approved first is the fastest way to speed everything up.

        What's my home worth?

        Online estimates can be off by tens of thousands. Pamela prepares a free local market analysis based on recent nearby sales, the condition of your home, and current demand. Request one in the form below.

        Why are property taxes so high in New Jersey?

        Property taxes pay for local schools and town services, so they vary a lot, even between neighboring towns. Pamela compares real tax bills before you make an offer, and eligible homeowners may qualify for state relief programs like ANCHOR.
